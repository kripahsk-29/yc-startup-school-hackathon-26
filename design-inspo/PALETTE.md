# Taste Test — Palette & Design Direction

Source: Pinterest board `in.pinterest.com/kripahsk/hackathon/` (14 pins, saved in this
folder as `pin-01.jpg` … `pin-14.jpg`) plus Poshitha's annotated Figma board
("I like this color red", "newspaper feel", "loading page with small ASCII art").

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--paper` | `#F2EDE3` | Dominant background — warm newsprint cream |
| `--paper-deep` | `#E7DFCF` | Recessed panels, alternating sections |
| `--ink` | `#171412` | Text / near-black ink, editorial blocks |
| `--accent` | `#96281B` | THE brick-lacquer red (user-picked) — headlines, the score, rules |
| `--accent-bright` | `#C8102E` | Small hot highlights only (ticks, live indicators) |
| `--line` | `#8B8170` | Hairline borders, plate frames, muted captions |

## Typography

- **Display:** high-contrast serif (newspaper masthead energy) — big, tight, some
  uppercase. Pins show didone-style red editorial type ("KARAOKE NIGHT", masthead
  layouts).
- **Labels / metrics:** monospace, uppercase, letterspaced — instrument-panel
  captions, figures, plate numbers, the score. ASCII-art flourishes welcome.
- Mix: serif carries the story, mono carries the measurements. No geometric sans.

## Texture / material

- Newsprint paper field; thin hairline rules and dotted borders (specimen plates,
  ledgers).
- Vintage laboratory / control-room B&W photography as plate imagery — operators at
  consoles, radio assembly lines, CRT walls. Duotone/grain over photos is on-brand.
- Black full-bleed editorial blocks (like the dasha u. / aino.agency pins) for
  contrast sections.
- Instrument-manual details: plate numbers ("FIG. 03"), crop marks, calibration
  ticks, stamped captions.

## Three adjectives

**Archival. Calibrated. Editorial.**

## v2 DIRECTION (2026-07-24, supersedes above styling)

Reference: https://craft.wild.as/ — full adoption per Poshitha.

- **Background:** near-white `#FBFBF9` with faint graph-paper grid
  (`rgba(14,14,16,0.05)` hairlines, ~28px cells).
- **Ink:** `#0E0E10`. Mono labels `#8A8A86`, uppercase, letterspaced.
- **Type:** grotesque sans (Geist) for everything; huge uppercase hero;
  mono (Geist Mono) for labels/figures. Serif retired on the landing.
- **Illustration language:** chunky pixel mosaics — heatmap blobs, pixel
  glyphs. Pixel palette: navy `#14183C`, cobalt `#3B4FE0`, amber `#F2A33C`,
  red `#C8102E`, brick `#96281B`, lime `#D8F24B`.
- **Accent:** brick red `#96281B` for links/deltas only.
- No cards, ever (hard rule). Whitespace + hairlines.
